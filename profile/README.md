## Who we are

Founded in 2023 as a [Eurobot](https://www.eurobot.org/) team, we've since grown into the robotics club of the School of Electrical Engineering at the University of Belgrade. Our members come from faculties across the university, building autonomous robots that bring together mechanics, electronics and software.

## Open source

The repositories here are public because RUR Robotics is committed to **free and open source software**. Firmware, electronics and tooling we build should be available for others to learn from, reuse and improve.

There is **more to come**. We'll publish more of our work once it's cleaned up and ready.
